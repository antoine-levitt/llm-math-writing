Provenance and purpose
======================

This is a narrowly retargeted version of statement-version/main.tex,
prepared at the user's request on 3 November 2026. Its argument and most
of its wording come from “Coherent electronic transport in periodic
crystals”, by Eric Cancès, Clotilde Fermanian Kammerer, Antoine Levitt
and Sami Siraj-Dine:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.01990v2
  https://doi.org/10.1007/s00023-021-01026-3

The original source is preserved in original/paper.tex. The original
extraction and the first statement adaptation are preserved in main.tex
and statement-version/main.tex respectively. Their provenance and earlier
changes are documented in NOTES.txt and statement-version-NOTES.txt.
The previous sources and PDFs were hashed before this adaptation; the
snapshot is in prompt-version-parent-hashes.json.

The user requested minimal changes to fit the theorem's scope while
keeping the prose style and exposition. This is an AI-assisted adaptation
of the published proof, not an untouched human manuscript. It was prepared
after the initial readability comparison and discussion of its fairness;
that timing should be retained in any account of the benchmark. No new
readability grade was requested or obtained for this version. No model
paper was opened while making these changes. The mathematical reviewer
worked from the two baseline sources without reading model papers or
grading reports.

prompt-version/ is a clean reading bundle: main.tex, main.pdf, refs.bib
and build.sh. Attribution, editorial notes, the source diff and build
scratch files are kept outside that directory, as in statement-version/.
Removing explicit provenance does not make the retained prose unrecognizable.

Changes relative to statement-version/
=======================================

1. The Liouvillian discussion is specialized to the Bloch fibers and the
   occupied/unoccupied splitting actually used in the proof. The general
   bounded-operator and finite-dimensional introductions, arbitrary
   spectral interval notation, and unused contour formula for the inverse
   are removed. The commutator interpretation and eigenbasis explanation
   are retained.

2. The propagation argument now estimates the form energy on a fixed time
   interval. The explicit full graph-norm/exponential bound and its
   interpolation step are replaced by a short Grönwall argument. The
   common-domain propagator construction and its appendix remain.

3. The original two-step Dyson expansion and its first-order integration
   by parts are retained. The stronger remainder C ε²t³(1+t³), for εt≤1,
   is replaced by C_T ε² on 0≤t≤T and 0≤ε≤1. The proof still controls the
   unbounded current, uniformly in k and independently of the band gap.
   The explicit ν₀, ν₁, ν₂, λ and η bookkeeping disappears. The four
   weighted operator bounds actually used are justified directly, including
   the commutator requiring V∈H¹. The unbounded Dyson identities are
   explicitly interpreted on D(h₀²), which contains the occupied states.

4. The limit ε→0 is taken once at the start of the conductivity proof,
   using the uniform finite-time estimates. All later calculations concern
   jᴸᴿ. The split into regions away from and near crossings, cancellation
   of boundary terms, reduction to two bands, and explicit Dirac integral
   are unchanged in substance. Repeated expansions and ε-remainders are
   removed. The limiting theorem and its order of limits are unchanged.

5. The short assertion Iᴿ(δ,t)=Iᑫ(δ,t)+O(δ) now includes its necessary
   uniform-in-time justification. This is the radial-energy substitution
   already recorded outside the previous reading bundle in
   statement-version-NOTES.txt. The oscillatory phase is handled exactly
   by ρ=Δ/(2v), rather than by a Taylor expansion in t.

6. Small local justifications accompany the retargeting: the puncture
   boundary in Stokes' formula is O(r); sums of occupied eigenvalues are
   differentiated as a trace, allowing internal band crossings; transition
   sums converge absolutely by Cauchy–Schwarz; regions are understood on
   the reciprocal torus. The phrase expanding “close to t=0” is corrected
   to “close to ε=0”.

The introduction, oscillator example, main statement, physical sign
convention, gauge transform, fixed arbitrary directions, proof order,
local two-band construction, explicit Dirac calculation, and both
technical appendices are retained. Only Reed–Simon is cited. The changes
do not attempt to match the length or structure of a generated paper.

The complete source comparison is in prompt-version-changes.diff.

Build and verification
=======================

Run bash baseline/prompt-version/build.sh from the project directory,
or bash build.sh inside prompt-version/. Scratch files are written to
prompt-version-build/, outside the reading bundle. The resulting PDF is
copied into prompt-version/.

The PDF has 18 pages (the previous version has 19). LaTeX and BibTeX
complete successfully; all source references resolve, with no duplicate
labels or overfull boxes. The inherited epstopdf warning only notes that
shell escape is disabled. PDF author, title, subject and keywords metadata
are empty. Previous baseline sources and PDFs are unchanged.

A separate mathematical review of the changed arguments found no new
defect or missing dependency. It checked the partial inverse, form-energy
estimate, Dyson domains and weighted remainder, limit interchanges,
puncture cancellation and uniform radial-energy substitution. The
surviving factors −i/(4π²) and iπ²/4 still give +1/16 per node, with the
same factors from the arbitrary fixed directions. Rendered pages containing
the revised remainder proof and Dirac approximation were also inspected.
