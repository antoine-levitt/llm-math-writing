Rough blind readability assessment
=================================

Primary result: one fresh, isolated GPT-6-Astra/max grader. Five manuscripts
were randomized as A–E; only their PDFs/text extracts, the theorem statement,
and the common grading rubric were supplied. The condition mapping was not
given to the grader. The assessment concerns readability, not mathematical
correctness. No manuscript was changed.

Rank  Condition                 Label  Score / 10
----  ------------------------  -----  ----------
1=    Explicit writing + review   A         9
1=    Explicit writing            E         9
3     Iterative review            C         8
4     Original prompt             D         7
5     Adapted baseline            B         6

The grader preferred A and E for making the central mechanism visible early
and linking estimates to their purpose. A's momentum-transport roadmap and
E's explanation of the filled-band cancellation were particular strengths.
C was clear and careful but required more bookkeeping. D devoted more of
the main narrative to operator preliminaries before revealing their payoff.
B's oscillator example earned its space, but the grader found its notation
and quantitative remainder estimates demanding and its final oscillatory
approximation disproportionately compressed.

Confidence was moderate. The leading pair was effectively tied; the C/D
ordering depended more on the reader's preference for technical detail.
These are coarse judgments from one isolated grading pass, not precise
measurements of quality or evidence of a mathematical error.

READABILITY_REVIEW_BLIND.txt is the grader's unchanged report, with two
located examples per manuscript. private-mapping.json records the labels,
source paths and source PDF hashes. anonymous-inputs/ retains the exact
anonymous papers/text supplied. isolated/ retains the exact prompt,
configuration, startup dump, sandbox test, native trace, usage and output.
verification.json records the final isolation/input checks.

Why there are two recorded passes
--------------------------------
The first attempt used a subagent requested with fork_turns="none". Inspection
showed that its startup still included the personal/project AGENTS.md text,
including the commissioner's name. It had no condition mapping, but was
not fully isolated from that profile. Its report and trace are retained in
subagent-pass/; it is not the primary result above.

The primary pass used a separate CLI execution, private Codex state, disabled
memories/project instructions, and a restricted neutral directory. The same
rubric and A–E labels were retained. The previous review and private mapping
were blocked by the sandbox, and the actual native startup was verified to
contain only the environment user message and the exact grading prompt.
Standard Codex system/developer instructions remained. Both passes happened
to give the same ordering, with different score calibration.
