- Restricted the basis-invariance assertion to the physical response and sum-rule formulas; stated that the absolute-value estimates hold in every basis.
- Supplied the absolute time-integral estimate needed for Fubini in the Cesàro calculation.
- Made uniform control up to the conical punctures explicit in the band-energy identity.
- Added reciprocal-lattice covariance, the graph-norm resolvent argument, and measurability of the fiber propagators.
- Matched the shifted resolvents to the evolution generator and explicitly proved norm conservation and weak uniqueness in the temporal gauge.
- Corrected formula punctuation, cross-reference names, and wording about operator ranges and translations.
- Checked the rendered PDF and kept the final lemma statement with its proof.
