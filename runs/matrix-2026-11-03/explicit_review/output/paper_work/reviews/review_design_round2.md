# Second editorial review

I reread the revised source, checked the compiled text, and visually inspected
pages 1, 3, 4, 7, 8, and 10 of
`paper_work/build/dirac_conductivity.pdf`.

**Assessment: the manuscript is ready after one small layout refinement.**
The revisions have resolved the meaningful problems in the first review.
Further rewriting would mostly trade one acceptable phrasing for another.

## Remaining layout refinement

Appendix A.2 begins at the bottom of page 8. Its Bloch-transform display
(A.5) is on page 8, but the sentence defining its measure and stating its
unitarity starts on page 9. Keep that opening block together, preferably by
starting A.2 on page 9. A page break before A.2 is defensible here: A.1
forms a complete unit, and the document will still be ten pages. Alternatively
reserve enough space for the heading, introduction, display, and the following
two lines. Inspect the rebuilt PDF after this change.

The partly filled final page is normal: it contains the end of the appendix
and the references. Do not compress the type or introduce further prose to
fill it.

## What now works well

* The introduction identifies the missing Fermi curve rather than relying
  on the weaker zero-area observation.
* The model's notation is introduced naturally, and both directional
  derivatives and velocities are defined.
* Proposition, lemma, section, and appendix references are correctly typed
  and capitalized in the PDF.
* The occupied-subspace result remains complete, while its technical use
  stays in the appendix. “Sobolev weights” is the right term.
* The characteristic formula now has a visible derivation. The reader can
  verify its sign and its source directly, without reconstructing an
  unstated argument.
* The real-space periodicity and reciprocal covariance are distinguished
  correctly.
* The cone argument explains why the eigenvalue hypotheses determine the
  physical current, and the radial/tangential interpretation is correctly
  restricted to the linearized cone.
* Renaming the scalar transition weight to `w(q)` removes the only notable
  collision with generic constants.
* The last proof page is particularly successful: the scaling, cancellation,
  dominator, elementary integrals, and final `M/16` conclusion are all visible
  together on page 7. The appendix begins cleanly on page 8.
* The explicit cross-cell trace-class argument closes the small analytic
  omission in the first draft without adding much notation.
* The bibliography and Reed--Simon applications remain accurate.

No figure is needed. The manuscript has a coherent path from the physical
question to the operator formula, then to the two-band geometry and finally
the universal constant. Its main statements are semantic units rather than
collections of proof ingredients. I recommend final compilation and delivery
after the A.2 opening is kept together.
